Who we are

This website is run by AQuadZ LLC, a company registered in the United States. You can reach us at asy@aquadz.com.

What this website collects

Nothing about you, unless you write to us. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites.

The company that hosts the site keeps standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. We don't use them to identify visitors.

When you email us

We use your name, email address and anything you include only to answer you and, if we work together, to run that relationship. Our email is provided by Google Workspace. We keep business emails for as long as we need them for the conversation and for our legal and accounting duties, and then delete them.

Our customers' data

Data that customers put into the apps we build and run for them belongs to those customers. We process it only on their instructions, under a written data processing agreement. This page covers this website only.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have these rights under the GDPR, and you can also complain to your data protection authority. Email asy@aquadz.com; we answer within 30 days.
