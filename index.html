Will the AI say something wrong to my customers?

The assistant answers only from your own information and rules, says so when it doesn't know, and hands over to a person. You decide which actions it may take alone and which need a person's approval.

Which AI do you use, and is my data safe?

We use leading AI models through business agreements under which your data is not used to train them. Your data is hosted in your region, encrypted, and exportable at any time.

Can it answer real phone calls and WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect the assistant to your phone number and your WhatsApp Business account, with natural voices and your opening hours, holidays and hand-over rules.

How long until we see results?

A focused pilot usually runs on live work within a few weeks. We agree the targets first, so you can see whether it pays for itself.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup for your data and integrations, then a monthly subscription based on usage. You get a fixed proposal after a short call.
